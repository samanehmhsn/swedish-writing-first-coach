01 / The opportunity

Fluent in your head.
Stuck in conversation.

Understanding a language and being able to use it are different skills. The brief was to design a coach for the space between them, with professional nuance intact.

01 — Observe

Recognition isn't retrieval.

A learner might know a word when they see it, but struggle to find it during a meeting or interview.

02 — Reframe

Write first. Correct second.

Make the learner produce their own language before offering suggestions, then make every change visible.

03 — Design

Practice where life happens.

From asking someone to watch a bag at a café to articulating a product strategy or an artistic practice.

03 / The curriculum

A language course built around a life, not a textbook.

Six modules, 24 lessons. Each lesson combines a conversation, a writing task, tracked corrections, imitation and active recall. Optional homework keeps progress flexible.

01 /3 lessons

Everyday interactions

Small requests, introductions, arriving somewhere, small talk.

02 /6 lessons

Job interviews

Professional identity, achievements, challenging questions, confidence.

03 /4 lessons

Marketing & business

Briefs, brand positioning, campaigns, results, correspondence.

04 /4 lessons

Product & strategy

Research, prioritization, stakeholders, launches, decision-making.

05 /5 lessons

Contemporary art

Artistic practice, critique, exhibition texts, grants and curators.

06 /2 lessons

Real-world simulations

Difficult conversations, negotiation, final integrated exercises.

The lesson sequence is a framework, not a cage. A real interview or grant application can become the lesson.

04 / Under the hood

The design is more than a clever prompt.

A portable, instruction-driven prototype with consistent teaching rules, structured materials and a transparent feedback model.

01

Instruction architecture

A reusable SKILL.md defines the learning philosophy, correction format, lesson sequence and tone. The coach separates errors from stylistic suggestions.

02

Content system

A 24-lesson curriculum, scenario prompts, session templates and fictional examples guide different modes of practice without making them rigid.

03

Continuity & recall

A structured learning-log template captures missing words, recurring mistakes and phrases to imitate. Persistent updates require a writable file integration.
